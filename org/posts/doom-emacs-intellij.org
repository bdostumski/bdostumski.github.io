" EDITING QUALITY OF LIFE
" ============================================================

" Q was mapped to gq, which IdeaVim does not implement. Replay @q instead.
nnoremap Q @q

" Yank to end of line, like D and C

" Keep the cursor parked while scrolling / searching / joining
nnoremap <C-d> <C-d>zz
nnoremap <C-u> <C-u>zz
nnoremap n nzzzv
nnoremap N Nzzzv
nnoremap J mzJ`z

" x and single-char deletes must not clobber the yank register
nnoremap x "_x

vnoremap K :m '<-2<CR>gv=gv

nnoremap ]<Space> o<Esc>k
